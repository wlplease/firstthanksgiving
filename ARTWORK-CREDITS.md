# Shore & Season original illustrations

Original food and place illustrations were generated for Shore & Season with OpenAI image generation and are shipped locally with the app, so recipe cards do not hotlink to image-search results or republish third-party photography.

- `public/florida-foodways.webp` — Florida wetlands, hearts of palm, pumpkin, and fry bread.
- `public/chesapeake-foodways.webp` — Chesapeake water, fish, shellfish, corn, and beans.
- `public/northeast-foodways.webp` — New England shore, clam broth, corn cakes, cranberries, and autumn vegetables.
- `public/harvest-foodways.webp` — East Coast harvest ingredients including corn, beans, squash, and cranberries.
- `public/rice-sofkee.webp` — Rice sofkee in a Florida wetland setting.
- `public/blueberry-dumplings.webp` — Blueberry dumplings.
- `public/cherokee-bean-bread.webp` — Bean bread dumplings made with cornmeal.
- `public/sochan-greens.webp` — A cooked leafy greens dish; not a plant identification image.
- `public/narragansett-strawberry-bread.webp` — Contemporary strawberry cornmeal bread.

Each recipe entry also has a dedicated triptych under `public/recipes/`. Each artwork shows that specific recipe’s ingredients, a cooking moment, and the finished dish. Recipe triptych files:
